## Findings checked by the lead

- `app/proof_checker/src/lib.fe` computes the terminal step from the public point
  and invokes `check_witness`; `witness.fe` reconstructs the trace and roots.
  This does check the public Q12 computation through replay. It is not a
  succinct verifier and does not hide the point.
- `composition.fe::check_composition` checks the supplied composition table's
  root and inverse-transform degree, without evaluating the AIR against source
  openings. `fri.fe` consumes LDE roots in the transcript without authenticating
  LDE row openings. Transcript binding does not establish this missing algebraic
  relationship. A second bounded source inspection independently agreed.
- Executed the shipped Fe/Wasm `preflight` at Q12 (-8192,0): it returns success
  at step1. Exact c=-2 has orbit 0,-2,2,2,..., so radius equality is not exact
  escape. This is the historical Q12 lane, not the new strict interval checker.
  The reproduction is `q12-preflight-reproduction.json` in the review directory.
